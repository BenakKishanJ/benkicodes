## Style
- Direct and practical. Explain like a friend who just figured it out.
- Answer first: the opening two sentences say what the reader will be able to do or know.
- Short paragraphs. Concrete examples before abstractions. Plain words over jargon.
- First person is fine, but never invent personal experience or results. Only use opinions/notes I supplied.
- Call out trade-offs and what I'd skip.
